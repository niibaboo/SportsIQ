This one works differently from the rest of the suite: it doesn't blend rolling form into a projection. It takes a bookmaker's own 1X2 odds and strips out their built-in margin ("de-vigging") to show the bookmaker's true implied Home/Draw/Away probabilities -- a market read, not a stats-driven prediction. Prices shown are Bet365's own price where Bet365 has posted odds on that match; where they haven't, it falls back to the average of whichever bookmakers have (labelled "market avg" below). Each bookmaker's last seen price is used, falling back to their opening price only if no later price was recorded. Where other bookmakers are also on a match, each tile adds a small "mkt" line comparing Bet365's price to the market average -- a de-vigged number on its own carries no edge, since it's just that book's own margin removed, so a highlighted gap there (Bet365 paying longer odds than the wider market thinks fair) is the part worth a second look, not the percentage itself. Tap a Home/Draw/Away tile on any Bet365-priced match to add it to the slip calculator at the bottom of the page. Not every upcoming match has odds posted yet -- those are left off rather than guessed at.
